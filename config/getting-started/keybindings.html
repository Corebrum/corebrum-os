Keybindings

Super+Space Learn then Keybindings lists these shortcuts. Choosing a row there closes the list. Volume, brightness, Print without Super, and the text console stay off this list.

PressAction
Super+ReturnOpen a terminal
Super+KClear the terminal screen
Control+SpaceTranscribe the microphone
Super+Shift+ReturnOpen Firefox
Super+Shift+FOpen Files
Super+Shift+NOpen Text Editor
Super+WClose the focused window
Super+QClose the focused window
Super+FFullscreen
Super+TFloat or tile the focused window
Super+CCopy
Super+XCut
Super+VPaste
Super+SpaceOpen the grouped application menu above fullscreen when needed. Rows with > open another level. Enter or Right opens a group; Backspace or Left goes up a level when the search box is empty
Super+Alt+SpaceOpen the app grid
Super+SOpen a floating Kitty terminal
Super+ADefault AI agent in a floating terminal (set under AI Agents when the CLI is installed)
Super+EscapeLock the screen. Type the account password and press Enter, or press Unlock
Super+Shift+EscapePause autolock, or resume it
Super+Shift+ELog out and return to the login screen
Super+Shift+CClipboard history
Super+PrintRecord the area, camera, or audio
Super+Shift+PrintStop recording (wait for Recording saved in Videos before opening the file)
Super+Print (while recording)Also stops the current recording if Super+Shift+Print does not register on your keyboard
Super+Control+PrintStop recording (alternate shortcut)
Super+PCopy a selected area
Super+Shift+PCopy the whole screen
Super+Alt+PRecord the area, camera, or audio (Super+Option+P on a Mac keyboard)
Super+Alt+Shift+PStop recording — only after the Recording. message (Super+Option+Shift+P on a Mac; wait for Recording saved in Videos before opening the file)
Super+ArrowsMove focus to the tile in that direction
Super+Shift+ArrowsSwap with the tile in that direction
Super+1 … Super+5Switch to that workspace
Super+Shift+1 … 5Move the focused window to that workspace
