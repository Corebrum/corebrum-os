RealSense · realsenseai.com

Super+Space RealSense installs librealsense, realsense-ros, and pyrealsense2. The first-login wizard keeps this step closed until ROS 2 is installed.

After the install, the same menu has two rows. RealSense Viewer launches realsense-viewer. RealSense ROS opens a terminal running ros2 launch realsense2_camera rs_launch.py.

corebrum camera list lists connected RealSense devices, or points at Super+Space when the camera stack is not installed.
